🏁 TPC Compliance, Scoring & Cut Line Rules

The TPC Driver Standings measures athlete intentionality, physical accountability, and daily nutritional discipline during off-season two-a-days.

Weekly Points Architecture (Max 295.0 pts/week):
  • 🏋️ S&C In-Gym Workouts (Max 162.0 pts): 6 sessions/week (Mon/Tue/Thu AM & PM). 10.0 pts attendance + up to 17.0 pts workout card fidelity (27.0 pts max per session).
  • 🧘 Wednesday Yoga & Recovery (Max 13.0 pts): 10.0 pts Yoga attendance + 3.0 pts Active Recovery (≥ 20 min logged in AMS Recovery Tracker).
  • 🏁 Friday Competition (Max 20.0 pts): 20.0 pts Event attendance via Teamworks Hub Event Check-In.
  • 💧 Stephanie's USG Hydration (Max 100.0 pts): Monday & Thursday testing. 25.0 pts sample submission + up to 25.0 pts hydration tier score (Tier 1: 25 pts, Tier 2: 15 pts, Tier 3: 10 pts, Tier 4: 5 pts). Max 50.0 pts/test day.
S&C Workouts (162 pts) + Yoga & Recov (13 pts) + Friday Comp (20 pts) + USG Hydration (100 pts) = 295 pts / wk ⚡ 80% Cut Line = 236.0 pts

📡 Remote / Traveling Workout & Polar Policy:

Off-site/remote athletes use Wayne's secondary asynchronous calendar track ("Two-a-Days Remote"). Remote attendance is split: 5.0 pts for logging the workout card + 5.0 pts for verified Polar Heart Rate tracking (Wayne verifies in AMS "S&C - Polar Heart Rate Verification") + up to 17.0 pts card fidelity. Unverified Polar HR tracking results in a 5.0 pt deduction.

🔑 Athlete Card & Coach Access

The timing & scoring board is public to drivers and gym screens, but individual workout card fidelity and Wayne's coaching notes are strictly private.
